Privacy Policy — Pressing Under Pressure

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Pressing Under Pressure ("the app") is a free single-player game supported by ads, published by t4zb technologies. The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or other personal details.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising identifier (on iOS the IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, coarse location, device and app information, diagnostics and how you interact with ads.

On iOS you choose whether apps may track you through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and you can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads and can change their choice later from the privacy button in the game's menu.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your best score, your sound and vibration settings and your total play time locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. We do not run our own servers and do not sell personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
